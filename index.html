Drag mode
IK: drag a hand or foot and the arm/leg follows; drag an elbow or knee to choose which way it bends (the hand/foot stays put).
FK: drag any joint to rotate that bone; everything below follows.
Free: move only that joint (bones stretch), for perspective.
IK/FK: drag the hips to move the whole body; the head, neck and shoulders rotate.
Double-click/tap a hand or foot (or P): pin it.
Alt-drag: a one-off Free drag in any mode.
Keys: I / F / G switch IK / FK / Free, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z redo, M mirror, R reset.
Input
Pose
Output
Grab a hand, a foot or the hips · double-click or double-tap to pin
